# Portraits das magias globais

Gerados com a ferramenta integrada `image_gen`, usando `apps/web/public/assets/command-portraits.png` como referência visual. Quatro chamadas independentes; sem CLI. Originais preservados no diretório de geração do Codex. Assets finais em WebP 512×512, redimensionados sem recorte, usados diretamente pelo HUD:

- `apps/web/public/assets/spell-bills.webp` — Chuva de boletos.
- `apps/web/public/assets/spell-recall.webp` — Volta pro grupo!
- `apps/web/public/assets/spell-silence.webp` — Modo avião.
- `apps/web/public/assets/spell-strength.webp` — Agora vai!

## Prompt comum (prefixo exato)

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one square 1:1 raster portrait for a Brazilian satirical RTS spell button, viewed at 58 pixels. The attached contact sheet is STYLE REFERENCE ONLY: match its thick dark ink outlines, rounded exaggerated proportions, warm hand-painted cel shading, gold highlights, crisp clean silhouettes, slightly worn tactile surfaces, dark olive-green vignette backdrop. Paint ONE centered large subject filling ~85% of square, leave bottom 12% quiet for UI price overlay. Not a contact sheet. No border, lettering, text, numbers, logos, watermark. Same production art direction as reference.

## Sufixos exatos de cada chamada

**bills:** Chuva de boletos: a dramatic swirling stack of cream paper bank payment slips with bold black barcode marks (not readable text), tumbling downward around a glowing gold lightning bolt. Comical bureaucratic storm. Strong large readable silhouette, few sheets, dynamic diagonal composition.

**recall:** Volta pro grupo: a chunky green military boot being magically pulled backwards into a swirling teal circular portal, golden backward-curving arrow framing the boot. Comical forced retreat. Main boot and portal large and readable.

**silence:** Modo avião: a chunky red-and-cream megaphone muzzled with a broad band of tan adhesive tape in an X over its mouth, surrounded by subtle purple magical sparks. Silenced military orders; comical. Large megaphone fills frame.

**strength:** Agora vai: an exaggerated muscular raised forearm with clenched fist, rolled green sleeve, golden energy radiating around fist, red-orange magical glow and a small burst of sparks. Comical exaggerated strength boost, no face. Fist large and readable.
