## Prompt de geração

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: 8-frame transparent character animation atlas for existing Brazilian satirical RTS. Attached units.png is STRICT visual style reference only. Match thick dark olive ink outlines, warm hand-painted cel-shading, big expressive heads, short chunky bodies, same cartoon caricature proportions and toy-like clean colors. Output exactly 1536x1024 landscape: 4 equal columns, 2 equal rows, eight separate 384x512 cells. Exactly one SAME complete character per cell. All face RIGHT, orthographic side/3-quarter view. Generous genuine transparent padding in every cell, no clipping. Feet aligned near 90% cell height. Frames 0-3 four distinct walking poses; frames 4-5 healing gesture with a glowing GREEN plus sign held above his medical bag; frames 6-7 comic defeat kneeling then lying on side, no gore. Subject: a friendly caricature of a Cuban male doctor, medium brown skin, short curly dark hair, neat moustache, expressive face, white lab coat over turquoise shirt, dark trousers and brown shoes, stethoscope and chunky cream medical bag with a GREEN cross. Small Cuban flag patch on coat sleeve. No red cross symbol. Same face and clothing all frames. True transparent alpha background, NO ground, shadow, glow haze outside the character/healing plus, colored rectangles, grid, labels, words, logo or watermark.

## Edição de transparência

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: attached 1536x1024 eight-frame game character atlas. Correct ONLY its alpha/transparency: remove ALL dark olive/brown/gray backdrop, haze, shadows, glow and rectangles outside the dark character outlines. Output exact same 1536x1024 canvas and 4-column 2-row grid. Preserve EVERY character, pose, face, prop, outline, color, pixel location, size and baseline exactly. Do not redraw, move, rescale, crop or add anything. All background pixels, including gaps between poses and around limbs, must be genuinely fully transparent (alpha zero). Keep solid character interiors fully opaque with clean antialiased edges. Preserve the green healing plus symbols but remove their diffuse glow haze. NO checkerboard image, no white backdrop, no ghost halo, no labels. Only the eight existing characters and their solid healing plus symbols should remain visible.
